# MockingBird -- homepage project card

**Title:** MockingBird

**One-liner:** A local-first wildlife observatory that turns camera footage into
a record you can trust -- AI suggests, you confirm.

**Summary (about 50 words):** MockingBird detects candidate wildlife visits from
backyard cameras or recorded video, shows the evidence with a labeled species
suggestion, and builds its Bird Log and analytics only from what a person
confirms. It runs entirely on your own computer: FastAPI, SQLite, React,
MegaDetector, and BioCLIP.

**Tags:** Computer vision, Local-first, FastAPI, React/TypeScript, ONNX Runtime,
Raspberry Pi, Human-in-the-loop ML
