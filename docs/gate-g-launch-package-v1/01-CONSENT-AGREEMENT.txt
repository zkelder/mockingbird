WHAT THIS IS

MockingBird is unreleased software. You are testing a private
pre-release build (version 0.1.0). It is not a released product and not
a public beta. {{OWNER_NAME}} is the only point of contact for support
and for anything you report: {{OWNER_CONTACT}}.

WHAT MOCKINGBIRD DOES WITH YOUR DATA

- MockingBird is Local Only by default. Your camera footage, database,
  and history stay on your own computer. There is no MockingBird cloud
  service, account, or relay.
- Nothing leaves your computer unless you turn on one of these yourself:
  1. Weather context (Open-Meteo). Off by default. Turning on the
     separate sunrise/sunset setting does not turn this on.
  2. Access from your phone or another device. Off by default. It needs
     a special start command (see the MockingBird guide) and still
     requires signing in. Only use it on a network you trust.
  Neither one turns on the other.
- Bird-sound identification (BirdNET) is NOT included in this build. It
  is never downloaded or installed for you. It is not part of this test.
- Camera login details (for example an RTSP password) that you enter are
  stored in PLAIN TEXT on your own computer. They are not encrypted.
  They are protected only by your normal Windows account and file
  permissions.
- Privacy Zones and Ignore Zones stop activity in that area from
  starting a Visit. They do NOT blur or remove that area from every
  image MockingBird keeps. Do not rely on a Zone as a guaranteed blur.
- Your camera may capture people (household members, visitors,
  delivery drivers) as well as wildlife. That footage stays on your
  computer like everything else.

WHAT I MAY ASK YOU TO SHARE, AND WHAT I WON'T

If you report a problem, I may ask for:
- a plain description of what happened;
- a MockingBird Doctor summary or a support bundle (see
  05-DIAGNOSTICS-AND-SUPPORT.txt). Both leave out camera passwords,
  pairing secrets, sign-in tokens, and all images/video;
- basic facts about your setup (Windows version, graphics card, camera
  type, roughly how many cameras).

I will NOT routinely ask for your database file, your camera images or
video, or your camera passwords. If one specific item is truly needed
to reproduce a problem, I will ask for that one item, explain why, and
you are free to say no.

WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES NOT PROMISE

- It does not guarantee correct species identification. It offers
  guesses for you to confirm or correct, and it will sometimes honestly
  say it is not sure rather than guess.
- It does not promise a fixed time for a Visit to appear. Typically
  about 80-115 seconds after the activity; occasionally several minutes
  during busy activity.
- It has no cloud dependency.
- It is a nature-observation tool. It is not a security camera or
  alarm system and has no security-alerting function.

HOW LONG YOUR REPORTS ARE KEPT

Anything you send me is kept at least until this private test and the
release preparation that follows it are finished, so it can be used to
fix real problems. You can ask me to delete anything you sent, at any
time, including after you stop taking part.

YOUR RIGHT TO STOP

You can stop taking part at any time, for any reason, without
explaining why. Your MockingBird data stays exactly where it already is,
on your own computer. I will delete anything you sent me if you ask.
